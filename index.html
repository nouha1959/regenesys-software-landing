Introducing LinkHand — one connected chart ecosystem for care teams.

Regenesys Software is a software development company introducing LinkHand, a business-to-business, all-in-one unified chart ecosystem: top-tier proprietary enterprise software that combines all of a patient's doctors' charts into one ecosystem, letting clinicians evaluate a patient thoroughly through a personalized 360-degree clinical system in a single interface.

How LinkHand works — in brief

Another doctor, hospital, or institution wishing to participate in a patient's health care can easily upload their files — drag and drop — into the temporary setup of the clinician's dashboard for handoff. That gives the current clinician as much available information as possible about their patient, so there are no mix-ups. All of the information for a single patient lives on a single dashboard, so the clinician in front of that patient can evaluate them thoroughly — and providers can work together instead of working around missing records.
