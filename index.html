Regenesys Software is a software development company introducing LinkHand, a business-to-business, all-in-one unified chart ecosystem: top-tier proprietary enterprise software that combines all of a patient's doctors' charts into one ecosystem, letting clinicians evaluate a patient thoroughly through a personalized 360-degree clinical system in a single interface.

How LinkHand works — in brief

Another doctor, hospital, or institution wishing to participate in a patient's health care can easily upload their files — drag and drop — into the temporary setup of the clinician's dashboard for handoff. That gives the current clinician as much available information as possible about their patient, and the latest tools, so there are no mix-ups — and it gives the patient an edge, so they are truly receiving the best and latest health care available.

LinkHand aims to make all doctors, hospitals, and institutions work together cohesively by sharing information — making their jobs exponentially easier, faster, and cheaper, and saving the time spent getting records and outsourcing tests, labs, and more. Because the vast information for a single patient is now on a single dashboard, it helps prescribers — and it exponentially helps the patient.
